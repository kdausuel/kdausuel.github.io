Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Athena is a personal home assistant that runs on its owner’s own computer. It is a private project used by one household. This policy explains what Athena does with data from Google when its owner connects it to Google Drive.

What Athena uses Google for

Only one thing: to keep an encrypted backup of Athena in the owner’s own Google Drive, and to restore from it.

What Athena can see

  • Athena asks Google only for access to files it creates itself and the folder the owner picks (Google’s “drive.file” permission). It cannot see, read or change any other files in the Drive.
  • It also receives the email address of the Google account, so the dashboard can show which account is connected.

How the data is handled

  • Backups are encrypted on the owner’s computer before they are uploaded, with a recovery passphrase only the owner knows. Google stores encrypted files it cannot read, and so does nobody else.
  • The Google sign-in is kept in the computer’s own secure password store, never in plain files.
  • Athena runs on the owner’s computer. No data from Google is sent to the developer, to any server, or to anyone else. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Athena’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Sign out in Athena’s dashboard (Cloud backup), or remove Athena at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either one ends Athena’s access.
  • The backup files are ordinary files in the owner’s Drive, in the folder “Athena Backup”, and can be deleted there at any time.
