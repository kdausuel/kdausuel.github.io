Athena

Athena is a personal home assistant. She runs on her owner’s own computer at home, listens for her name, helps with lights, music, timers and reminders, and remembers what matters to the household.

Athena is a private project for one household. It is not a public service, there is nothing to sign up for, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Google Drive backup

Athena can keep an encrypted backup of herself in her owner’s Google Drive, so she can be restored on a new computer. She only ever sees the files she creates there, and everything is encrypted on the owner’s computer before it is uploaded.
